# Backgrounds

The three title-slide backgrounds from the 2026 corporate overview template, 1920 × 1080 JPEG. TI's circuit-trace and facet motif; use as supplied, never redrawn, tiled, cropped to a strip or placed behind body copy.

| file | look | title colour on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `title-grey-circuit.jpg` | white-to-light-grey facets with grey circuit traces, denser top-right and bottom-right | `title` red or `ink-strong`, top-left or bottom-left |
| `title-light-facets.jpg` | near-white facets, faint traces, the quietest of the three | `title` red, anywhere left of centre |
| `title-teal-circuit.jpg` | light facets left, teal (`teal-deck-300` range) wash and traces right | `title` red bottom-left on the light area; white only where the teal is dense and checked at 4.5:1 |

Cover (L01), section dividers (L02) and the closing (L30) only. Content slides are plain white.
